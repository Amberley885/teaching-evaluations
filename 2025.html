Student course evaluation, anonymous

Advanced Statistics, Summer 2025

Instructor: Jiewen Luo. Students rated the teaching highly, and the one recurring concern was fitting a wide range of material into two weeks.

Note on Course Evaluations. No official university course evaluation was conducted for this course, so I designed and administered an end-of-course survey using Google Forms. The survey was distributed to all enrolled students at the end of the course. Participation was voluntary, and responses were anonymous. The original survey results are available upon request.
